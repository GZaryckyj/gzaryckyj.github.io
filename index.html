Our belief

Meaningful connection has become rare, and more valuable than ever. People are craving deeper connection, intentional experiences and a stronger sense of belonging. Welda exists to bring people back to themselves, and to each other.

Our story

A lifestyle movement for better weekends.

Welda Club is a lifestyle wellness community bringing together ambitious, like-minded people through thoughtfully curated events, retreats and experiences designed to foster genuine connection and lasting relationships.

Whether it's a weekend gathering in South Florida, a rooftop morning in New York or a retreat somewhere far away, every Welda experience is designed to create space for presence, encourage growth and cultivate connection. Rooted in presence, not perfection.

Retreats

Step away. Come back to yourself.

Following the success of our inaugural retreat in Jamaica, we're expanding into immersive destination experiences designed to create deeper connection, meaningful transformation and unforgettable memories.

  • Thoughtfully curated. From accommodations and transportation to wellness programming and local experiences, every detail is planned so you can be fully present from the moment you arrive.
  • Nourishing dining. Shared, locally inspired meals become some of the most memorable moments of the trip.
  • Beyond wellness. Movement and mindfulness, plus adventure, conversation, creativity and culture.
  • Community-led. Every guest is invited to contribute something: a journaling session, a favorite recipe, a skill, a discussion or a personal ritual.
  • Building something bigger. Bringing together extraordinary people from different backgrounds, cities and industries to learn from one another.

Our approach

  1. Hospitality first

    Long before Welda, we built careers in hospitality, events and partnerships. Every experience gets the attention to detail, guest experience and service luxury brands are known for.

  2. End-to-end execution

    From venue sourcing and partner outreach to event marketing, guest communications, content production and on-site coordination, we manage every detail from concept to completion.

  3. Intentional alignment

    We partner selectively with brands, hotels and venues that genuinely complement our community. Every collaboration should feel like a natural extension of the experience.

What partners get

  • Content that lives beyond the event. Every event is professionally photographed and filmed from a shared shot list, with the assets delivered afterward for both Welda and our partners.
  • Elevated brand experiences. Meaningful touchpoints that feel natural, memorable and aligned with the overall guest experience.
  • Quality over quantity. Intimate, highly curated gatherings people genuinely want to attend, with the right people, partners and spaces.
