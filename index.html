Our belief

People are craving something deeper. Not another class to check off, but real presence, real conversation and real connection. Welda exists to bring people back to themselves, and to each other.

Our story

A lifestyle movement for better weekends.

We're redefining what it means to live well, for people who want deeper connection and slower living. Rooted in presence, not perfection or restriction.

Whether it's a weekend gathering in Miami, a rooftop morning in New York or a retreat somewhere far away, every Welda experience is designed to create space for presence, encourage growth and cultivate connection.

What we're about

Four things every Welda experience shares

01

Move

Pilates, yoga, padel, sunrise flows and breathwork. Movement that feels good, never punishing.

02

Connect

Thoughtful conversation, journaling and new friends. You arrive solo and leave with a community.

03

Explore

City rooftops, private beaches and, soon, destinations further afield. Adventure is part of the practice.

04

Belong

A club in spirit: like-minded people who show up for themselves and for each other.
